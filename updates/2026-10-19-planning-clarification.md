# Two more complete examples for everyday readers

The everyday path now includes [planning within fixed constraints](../everyday/planning-clarification/planning.md) and [clarifying a vague workshop request](../everyday/planning-clarification/clarification.md).

Three actual responses are retained. The planning response fits required work, a fixed break, and an unused buffer into 90 minutes while excluding the optional task. The clarification response asks about the goal and waits; a frozen scripted user reply then supplies the constraints for a 60-minute agenda.

Each page includes complete input, exact request, unedited response, arithmetic/factual checks, and an explicitly authored failure illustration. The checks were performed by the authoring assistant, not independent participants. These are demonstrations rather than a comparative prompt benchmark or a real workshop study.

An offline integrity checker reconstructs the exact requests and verifies input/output hashes. Semantic checks remain visible on the pages rather than being claimed as automatically proven by a hash check.
