# Research a claim

**Use:** For a new technique or product claim.

## Copy and adapt

```text
Investigate [exact claim]. Find and open primary sources. Record publication or version date and retrieval date. Explain the setting, method, findings, and limitations. Separate source statements from your inference and unresolved questions. Include direct citations. Propose a practical experiment only if it tests the claim.
```

## Check the result

Every consequential claim is supported by the cited passage.

## Watch for

A search snippet treated as a read paper.

Start with the shortest version that communicates the task. Add examples when ambiguity affects the result.
